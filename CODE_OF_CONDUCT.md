# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in Git'o welcoming, harassment-free, and
inclusive for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender
identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, race, religion, sex,
sexual orientation, or socioeconomic status.

## Our Standards

Examples of positive behavior include empathy, respect, constructive feedback,
accepting responsibility, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or imagery, harassment,
trolling, insults, personal attacks, publishing private information without
permission, and other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional
setting.

## Enforcement

Report abusive or unacceptable behavior privately through the
[GitHub Security Advisory form](https://github.com/overengineered-org/gito-vscode-extension/security/advisories/new).
Community leaders will review reports promptly and fairly, protect reporter
privacy, and may remove or reject content or restrict participation when
needed.

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and when someone represents the
project publicly. It is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
